# 🧱 Stage 1: The Foundation. What problem are we solving?

## The business
**Khass Designs and Prints** is a printing business in **Lapai, Niger State**. It makes gift boxes, magic cups, branded shirts, flyers, posters and banners, business cards, invitation cards, paper bags, jotters, screen prints and general printing. Its customers are student associations, departments, faculties and individuals.

## The problem
Most of the raw materials (flex rolls, school kits, blank shirts, paper) are bought in **Lagos**, but the printing happens in **Lapai**, a long road trip away (Lagos → Lapai → Minna → Abuja).

- Trucks and parcel services were slow and unpredictable.
- Finding suppliers the business could **trust** was hard.
- When materials arrived late, printing started late, and customer orders arrived late.

## My role
I live in Lagos and act as the **middleman and Logistics Manager**: I find and vet suppliers, choose carriers, and coordinate inbound materials and outbound finished orders. This project is the data behind that work.

## The questions the dashboard answers
| # | Question | Who cares |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How long does a delivery from Lagos to Lapai really take, and is it getting better? | Owner, operations |
| 2 | Are customer orders arriving on time? If not, why? | Owner, customers |
| 3 | What does it cost to deliver one order, and is that going down? | Owner, finance |
| 4 | Do our suppliers deliver what we ask for? | Owner, purchasing |

## What "success" looks like
Faster trips, more on-time orders, a lower cost per order and suppliers that deliver what they promise. The four KPIs are defined precisely in [`kpi-definitions.md`](kpi-definitions.md).

> **Note:** all data in this repo is **simulated** for a portfolio project, built to model a realistic improvement story.
